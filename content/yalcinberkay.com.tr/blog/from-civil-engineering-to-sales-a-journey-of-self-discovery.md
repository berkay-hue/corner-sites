I moved from civil engineering to sales after realising engineering was not my calling. Joining AIESEC to gain international experience became a turning point and a journey of self-discovery. Finding that I enjoyed communication and working with people led me into a sales career. My lesson: career paths are rarely linear, so stay open to change.

As a university student, I embarked on a path in Civil Engineering, initially envisioning a future in Architecture for my MBA. However, as I delved deeper into my studies, I realized civil engineering wasn't my calling. This moment of uncertainty led me to AIESEC, a decision initially motivated by the desire to gain international experience. Little did I know, this journey with AIESEC would change my life forever. I found myself thoroughly enjoying interactions with people, thriving in environments that required communication and connection. This realization was a pivotal moment for me, sparking a transformation in my career goals. I shifted gears, moving away from engineering to pursue a career in sales, starting where many sellers do before [moving from SDR to Account Executive](/blog/move-from-sdr-to-account-executive). This wasn't just a job change; it was a transition towards a role that resonated with my newfound passion and skills. My story is a testament to the evolving career mindset among younger generations. We're not just job-hopping; we're in pursuit of what truly drives us, even if it means exploring uncharted territories. Embracing change, I've found a career that aligns with my passions and strengths, and I encourage others to be open to the possibilities their own journeys might reveal.

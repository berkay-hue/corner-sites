## FAQ

### Why is financial literacy important in Turkey?

Financial literacy matters in Turkey because the country's unique economic landscape brings both challenges and opportunities. Understanding financial concepts helps people make better personal decisions, prepares them for uncertainty and contributes to a stronger, more resilient economy.

### What are the key areas of financial literacy?

The key areas are managing personal and household finances, understanding investment options and their risks, and using credit wisely. Together they help people make informed decisions rather than just understand numbers.

### How does financial literacy help with economic uncertainty?

Financial literacy prepares people for both financial uncertainties and opportunities. Understanding financial concepts makes it easier to navigate Turkey's economic landscape and make better personal decisions.

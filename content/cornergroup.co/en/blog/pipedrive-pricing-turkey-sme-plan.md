For most SMEs in Türkiye, the most sensible Pipedrive plan is the **mid-tier plan** (Advanced under the old naming, Growth under the new). It covers email sync, core automations and the day-to-day needs of a 2–10 person sales team at a reasonable per-user cost. Solo consultants can get by with the entry-level plan. If you need sales forecasting, quote and document management or advanced reporting, you'll need to move up a tier. The top-tier plans are generally suited to teams of more than 15–20 users with clearly defined permission and security requirements.

That said, you shouldn't choose a plan based on list price alone. Dollar- or euro-based billing, currency fluctuations, tax obligations tied to purchasing services from abroad, add-ons and the need to integrate with local accounting software all significantly change the real cost an SME in Türkiye ends up paying. In this guide, we walk step by step through Pipedrive's pricing structure, its hidden cost items, and which plan is right for different SME profiles, and why.

## How does Pipedrive pricing work?

Pipedrive runs on a **per-user monthly subscription** model. A few key variables determine the amount on your invoice:

- **Plan tier:** Each higher plan adds new capabilities on top of the features of the one below it.
- **Number of users:** A separate license is paid for every person who logs into the system. For a 5-person team, the price is multiplied by 5.
- **Billing period:** Paying annually upfront provides a noticeable discount compared with monthly billing. The difference varies by plan but is generally in the 15–20% range.
- **Add-ons:** Modules such as lead capture, email campaigns, website visitor tracking, document management and project tracking may be charged separately, depending on the plan.
- **Currency:** For subscriptions from Türkiye, prices are generally set in dollars or euros. Your credit card is charged the equivalent amount in Turkish lira.

Pipedrive generally offers a **14-day free trial** and doesn't ask for card details during the trial. This period is your most valuable resource when choosing a plan. We explain how to make the most of it separately below.

**Important note:** Pipedrive periodically restructures its plan names and package contents. For a long time, it used the Essential, Advanced, Professional, Power and Enterprise structure. More recently, it has moved to new names such as Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate. The price ranges in this article reflect approximate list prices at the time of writing. Always check the current price on Pipedrive's official pricing page before you buy.

## What do the plans offer?

Even though the plan names change, Pipedrive's packaging logic stays the same: four tiers, namely **entry, mid, professional and enterprise**. Below, we assess each tier from the perspective of an SME in Türkiye.

### Entry-level plan (Essential / Lite)

With annual billing, it starts at around **$14–15** per user per month.

**What's included:**
- Visual sales pipeline and deal management
- Contact and company records
- Activity and calendar management
- Basic reporting
- Mobile app

**What's missing:**
- Full two-way email sync (may be limited depending on the plan version)
- Automation (workflow) setups
- Features that boost a salesperson's daily productivity, such as email templates and open tracking

**Who it's for:** Solo consultants, freelance salespeople, or 1–2 person teams moving off Excel for the first time who want to build "pipeline discipline" first.

### Mid-tier plan (Advanced / Growth)

With annual billing, it ranges from about **$24–39** per user per month. The price varies depending on which period's package structure applies.

**What's added:**
- Full email sync with Gmail and Outlook
- Email templates, open and click tracking
- Visual automation builder (e.g., "create a follow-up task 3 days after a deal moves to the proposal stage")
- Group emails and scheduled sending

**Who it's for:** B2B sales teams of 2–10 people. For most SMEs in Türkiye, this plan is the **"sweet spot."** If a salesperson's emails don't automatically land in the CRM, the habit of logging activity never takes hold. That is exactly the feature that makes this plan valuable.

### Professional-tier plan (Professional / Premium)

With annual billing, it ranges from about **$49–59** per user per month.

**What's added:**
- Revenue forecasting and advanced reporting
- Quote and contract templates, e-signature (Smart Docs-type modules)
- Team-based goal management
- More advanced automation conditions
- In some package structures, lead capture or campaign modules are included

**Who it's for:** Teams of 5–25 people where the sales manager runs the team against targets, needs to produce forecasts for weekly pipeline meetings, and wants to standardize the quoting process inside the CRM.

### Enterprise-tier plans (Power, Enterprise / Ultimate)

With annual billing, they range from about **$64–99** per user per month.

**What's added:**
- Advanced permission and visibility settings
- Project management modules (depending on the package)
- Higher automation and custom field limits
- Priority support and security features

**Who it's for:** Companies managing multiple sales teams, regions or brands that need to restrict data access by role. For a typical 10-person SME, this tier is usually an unnecessary expense.

## How much does it cost? Calculating the true total cost

The list price is only one part of what you'll actually pay. Let's work through a concrete example.

**Example scenario:** An Istanbul-based SME selling industrial equipment, with 5 sales reps and 1 sales manager. It chooses the mid-tier plan with annual billing.

1. **License cost:** Let's assume $34 per user per month. 6 users × $34 × 12 months = **$2,448/year**.
2. **Add-on cost:** Assume a lead capture add-on is purchased to collect inquiries from the website. Add-on prices vary with usage volume. An extra line item of a few hundred dollars a year should be added to the budget.
3. **Tax impact:** For digital services purchased from a provider abroad, the company in Türkiye may be required to declare VAT as the responsible party (reverse charge). While this doesn't necessarily mean a direct cost increase, it affects cash flow and the accounting process. Be sure to clarify this with your accountant.
4. **Currency risk:** With monthly billing, you pay at a different exchange rate every month. Paying annually upfront locks in the cost at that day's rate. During periods when the exchange rate is rising, this is a significant advantage.
5. **Setup and integration:** Designing pipeline stages, migrating data, building automations and integrating with your accounting software all require either internal resources (time) or an external consulting budget.
6. **Training and adoption:** This is the most overlooked item. If the team doesn't use the CRM, the license fees are completely wasted.

In this example, while the license cost alone is around $2,500 a year, the total first-year cost including add-ons and setup can easily reach 1.5–2x that figure. ## Additional cost and compliance items for SMEs in Türkiye

Pipedrive is a global product. When it meets the operational realities of Türkiye, some issues need to be planned for separately.

### Accounting and ERP integration

Pipedrive generally doesn't have a ready-made, built-in integration with local accounting software such as Logo, Mikro or Netsis. If you want a won deal to automatically turn into a customer account record or a proforma invoice, you'll need a middleware solution. Here, **open-source automation tools like n8n** can be more economical in the long run than usage-based alternatives like Zapier. We described in detail how this kind of project is structured in our [CRM integration with Logo and Mikro case study](/blog/logo-mikro-crm-integration-sme-case-study).

### WhatsApp and phone usage

In Türkiye, a significant share of B2B sales still happens over the phone and WhatsApp. WhatsApp and VoIP integrations are available in Pipedrive's app marketplace, but most of them are third-party products that charge a **separate subscription fee**. Account for this item separately when planning your budget.

### Language and support

Pipedrive's interface offers a Turkish language option, which makes team adoption easier. However, support channels operate mainly in English. Designating a "CRM owner" on your team who can communicate in English on technical matters helps resolve issues quickly.

### KVKK and data storage

Keeping customer data in a system hosted abroad requires an assessment of cross-border data transfer under KVKK. Review your privacy notices and explicit consent processes within this framework. It's best to proceed together with your legal advisor.

## Which plan should which SME choose?

In theory, every plan can seem suitable for everyone. In practice, the decision becomes clear based on team structure and the complexity of your sales process.

### Solo consultancy or agency

- **Recommended plan:** Entry level
- **Why:** A small number of open deals, personal calendar management and a simple pipeline are sufficient.
- **When to upgrade:** When you start tracking more than 30–40 open deals at once, or when email tracking becomes critical.

### B2B inside sales team of 3–8 people

- **Recommended plan:** Mid tier
- **Why:** Email sync and automated follow-up tasks largely solve the team's "forgotten deal" problem. Thanks to automation, the sales manager is freed from the burden of manual checks.
- **When to upgrade:** When the sales manager needs to produce monthly and quarterly forecasts and send quotes from within the CRM using standard templates.

### Mixed field and inside sales team of 10–25 people

- **Recommended plan:** Professional tier
- **Why:** Goal management, revenue forecasting and advanced reporting make it possible to compare the performance of multiple salespeople. For the field team, the mobile app and location-based activity planning come to the fore.
- **Watch out:** Instead of moving the whole team to a higher plan just for reporting, handling reporting with an external BI tool can also be an option. However, Pipedrive generally requires a single plan to be applied company-wide, so a mixed-license setup is usually not possible.

### Dealer network or multi-brand structure

- **Recommended plan:** Professional or enterprise tier
- **Why:** If dealers or brands must not see each other's data, advanced visibility and permission settings become essential.
- **Alternatives to consider:** Companies with this structure should also compare the multiple pipeline and permission options of HubSpot or Zoho.

## Step-by-step decision process for choosing a plan

To choose the right plan, we recommend following this sequence:

1. **Map out your sales process on paper.** How many stages are there from a lead's first contact to invoicing? Who does what at each stage? Without this map, you can't know which features you need.
2. **Make a "must-have" and "nice-to-have" list.** For example, email sync might be a must-have, while e-signature might fall into the nice-to-have category.
3. **Determine your real number of users.** Only give licenses to people who actively manage deals. For a general manager who wants to see reports once a month, an exported report or a shared dashboard may be sufficient instead of a full license.
4. **Start the trial with the mid or professional plan.** Starting with a higher plan lets you see which features you actually use. If there are features you don't need at the end of the trial, you can move down to a lower plan.
5. **Work with real data during the trial.** Bring at least 20–30 real deals and two salespeople into the system. A trial run on an empty account is misleading.
6. **Calculate the total cost.** Combine license, add-on, integration, setup and training costs in a single table.
7. **Start with monthly billing, then switch to annual.** Pay monthly for the first 1–2 months to see how the team adopts it. Once the system is up and running, switch to annual billing for the exchange rate advantage and discount.

## Ways to use Pipedrive at a lower cost

There are some practical ways for budget-conscious SMEs to save:

- **Pay annually:** It provides both a discount and the advantage of locking in the exchange rate.
- **Give licenses only to those who actually use them:** Close the licenses of departing employees immediately. An unused license quietly drains your budget every month.
- **Buy add-ons when the need arises:** Don't purchase modules such as campaigns or website visitor tracking upfront on the basis of "we might use it."
- **Build automation externally:** When Pipedrive's built-in automation limits aren't enough, building external automation workflows with n8n can be cheaper than upgrading to a higher plan. For example, a workflow that enriches an inquiry from a web form and assigns it to the right salesperson can be set up in just a few steps with n8n.
- **Evaluate the AI features:** Pipedrive's AI-powered sales assistant and email suggestions are included in some plans. Test what's included in your plan before paying for a separate AI tool.
- **Watch for promotional periods:** Pipedrive may occasionally offer seasonal discounts to new customers. Don't delay a critical decision to wait for them, but take them into account if your timing is flexible.

## Common mistakes

These are the most common mistakes SMEs in Türkiye make when choosing Pipedrive:

- **Starting with the cheapest plan and saying "we'll upgrade later":** On a plan without email sync, the team sees the CRM as extra workload, and adoption never gets off the ground.
- **Giving everyone a license:** Licenses opened for accounting, operations and management that never get used waste a significant portion of the budget.
- **Underestimating setup:** An account opened without designing pipeline stages, required fields and loss reasons turns into a chaotic data dump within a few months.
- **Ignoring currency risk:** With monthly billing, the budget changes unpredictably along with exchange rate movements.
- **Leaving integration until last:** If data doesn't flow between accounting and the CRM, salespeople enter the same information twice and record-keeping discipline breaks down.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Pipedrive have pricing specific to Türkiye?

Pipedrive generally doesn't offer a Türkiye-specific price list in Turkish lira. Prices are set in dollars or euros, and your card is charged the equivalent in lira. For this reason, paying annually upfront is the most practical way to lock in your cost against exchange rate fluctuations.

### Is Pipedrive's entry-level plan sufficient for a small team?

For solo operators or teams with a very simple sales process, the entry-level plan may be sufficient. However, if there are two or more salespeople and customer communication runs largely over email, the mid-tier plan, which offers email sync and automation, is usually a more productive investment.

### Can different plans be assigned to different users in Pipedrive?

Pipedrive generally requires a single plan to be applied across the company account. In other words, assigning one user to the professional plan and another to the entry plan isn't possible in most cases. For this reason, you need to choose your plan based on the team's overall needs rather than the user group with the greatest needs.

### Does Pipedrive integrate with accounting software like Logo or Mikro?

There is generally no built-in, ready-made integration. However, using automation tools like n8n via Pipedrive's API, you can set up workflows such as transferring won deals to a customer account record or a proforma invoice. These kinds of integrations require technical planning, but when set up correctly, they eliminate double data entry.

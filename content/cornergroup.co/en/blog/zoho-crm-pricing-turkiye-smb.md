**For most SMBs in Türkiye, the most sensible starting point is Zoho CRM's Standard plan. For teams with a defined sales process, more than 5 people and a need for automation, the Professional plan offers the best price-to-performance balance.** The Enterprise and Ultimate plans only justify their cost when you have multiple sales teams, territory management needs or advanced customization requirements. Teams of up to 3 people can get started with the free plan or with Bigin, Zoho's simplified CRM.

Making this decision based on list price alone is the most common mistake SMBs make. In Türkiye, currency fluctuations, the difference between annual and monthly billing, VAT obligations, and setup and integration costs can significantly change the total budget. In this guide, we cover Zoho CRM's plans, realistic cost calculations and which plan fits which SMB profile, using concrete scenarios.

## What are Zoho CRM's plans and how much do they cost?

Zoho CRM is offered in five core tiers. The prices below are **approximate reference values** based on Zoho's international list prices. Since Zoho may update its pricing by region, currency and period, we recommend checking the official pricing page before you buy.

| Plan | Billed annually (per user/month) | Billed monthly (per user/month) | Who is it for? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Up to 3 users, trials and very small teams |
| Standard | ~$14 | ~$20 | SMBs new to CRM that need a basic pipeline |
| Professional | ~$23 | ~$35 | Growing teams that want process automation and quote management |
| Enterprise | ~$40 | ~$50 | Companies with multiple teams, territories or a dealer network |
| Ultimate | ~$52 | ~$65 | Organizations that need advanced analytics and higher limits |

### What does the Free plan include?

The free plan includes lead, contact, account and deal management, basic task tracking and the mobile app. It has clear limits on record counts, automation and reporting. This plan is a good tool for answering the question "Is a CRM right for our team?", but it won't sustain a serious sales operation for long.

### What does the Standard plan add?

The Standard plan provides the core features SMBs need for day-to-day sales operations:

- Multiple sales pipelines
- Lead scoring
- Basic workflow rules
- Customizable dashboards and reports
- Automatic lead capture from your website via web forms
- Mass email
- Sales forecasting

### Why is the Professional plan the "sweet spot"?

The Professional plan introduces **Blueprint** process management, one of Zoho's strongest differentiators. With Blueprint, you can require a quote file to be uploaded before a deal can move to the "Quote Sent" stage, or require manager approval when a discount exceeds a certain threshold. This plan also includes quote, sales order and invoice modules (inventory management), validation rules and higher automation limits. For B2B companies with complex quoting processes, this is where the difference begins.

### Who needs Enterprise and Ultimate?

The Enterprise plan offers territory management, more comprehensive features of the Zia AI assistant, customer journey design, a sandbox environment and profile-based page layouts. Ultimate adds advanced business intelligence (BI) and higher data limits on top of these. Apart from manufacturers managing a dealer network or companies running separate sales teams for different product lines, these plans are a premature investment for most SMBs.

## Why might Zoho CRM prices look different in Türkiye?

The price you see when you visit Zoho's website from Türkiye may differ from the price a colleague sees from another country. There are several reasons for this.

**1. Regional pricing:** Zoho may apply region-specific pricing in some markets. Pay attention to which currency the price is displayed in and which currency you will be invoiced in.

**2. Currency risk:** With a dollar- or euro-based subscription, be sure to factor in exchange rate changes when converting your annual budget plan to Turkish lira. Paying annually upfront is the most practical way to lock in this risk for a year.

**3. VAT obligations:** When you purchase services from a software provider based abroad, how VAT is declared depends on your company's structure. For corporate purchases, a reverse-charge VAT declaration may come into play. Clarify this with your financial advisor before buying, as it can otherwise show up as an unexpected line item in your budget.

**4. Annual vs. monthly billing:** As the table above shows, monthly billing is noticeably more expensive per user than annual billing. The difference is between 30 and 50 percent on most plans.

**5. Buying through a local partner:** When you purchase through Zoho partners in Türkiye, setup, training and support packages may be added to the license price. While this increases the total cost, a properly designed implementation prevents the cost of inefficiencies down the road.

## What is the total cost of ownership?

The license fee is just the tip of the iceberg. For a realistic budget, adapt the following calculation to your own team.

### Sample calculation: 8-person sales team, Professional plan

- **Annual billing:** 8 users × $23 × 12 months = **$2,208/year**
- **Monthly billing:** 8 users × $35 × 12 months = **$3,360/year**
- **Difference:** $1,152 per year. In other words, your billing choice alone can save enough to cover the annual licenses of nearly 4 additional users.

### Cost items beyond licensing

1. **Setup and configuration:** Designing pipeline stages, fields, automations and permission profiles. If you do it in-house, there is an internal time cost; if you work with a partner, there is a project fee.
2. **Data migration:** Cleaning and transferring data from Excel, a legacy CRM or accounting software. Removing duplicate records usually takes longer than expected. If you're curious how this process plays out in real life, take a look at [our case study of an SMB that moved from Excel to a CRM](/blog/sme-excel-to-crm-migration-case-study).
3. **Integrations:** Integrations with accounting software, e-invoicing systems, website forms and phone systems. Ready-made integrations with local accounting software may not always be available. In that case, you'll need to build a bridge using automation tools like n8n or custom development.
4. **Add-ons:** Extra storage, additional record limits, telephony integration or Zoho Marketplace apps may be charged separately.
5. **Training and adoption:** Training and follow-up to ensure the sales team actually uses the CRM. Most CRM projects fail not for technical reasons, but because of low adoption.

**Rule of thumb:** When calculating your first-year budget, set aside a separate line item for setup, integration and training on top of the license fee. The size of this item depends on the complexity of your process, but assuming it is zero is almost always a mistake.

## Which plan suits which SMB?

Let's set theory aside and look at concrete scenarios based on typical SMB profiles in Türkiye.

### Scenario 1: 3-person consulting firm

A founder and two sales consultants. 20–30 new conversations per month, a 2–4 week sales cycle and a simple quoting process.

- **Recommendation:** Start with a 1–2 month trial on the Free plan, then move to Standard or Bigin if needed.
- **Why:** This team needs a visible pipeline and follow-up reminders. Blueprint or territory management is unnecessary.
- **Approximate cost (Standard, annual):** 3 × $14 × 12 = $504/year

### Scenario 2: 8-person B2B software sales team

2 SDRs (lead qualification), 5 sales reps, 1 sales manager. A 1–3 month sales cycle with demo and proposal stages, and discounts subject to approval.

- **Recommendation:** Professional plan.
- **Why:** SDR-to-sales rep handoff rules, discount approval workflows and the quotes module are critical for this team. The Standard plan's automation limits will quickly fall short.
- **Approximate cost (annual):** $2,208/year

For teams like this, a CRM's value is directly tied to the quality of the leads going into it. ### Scenario 3: 25-person manufacturer working with a dealer network

Regional managers, a field sales team, inside sales and dealer management. Different price lists and permission levels across regions.

- **Recommendation:** Enterprise plan (at least for managers and regional leads).
- **Why:** Territory management, profile-based visibility and complex permission structures are limited in the Professional plan.
- **Approximate cost (annual):** 25 × $40 × 12 = $12,000/year

### Mixed licensing strategy

Zoho does not always allow different plans to be assigned to different users within the same account. Generally, an account runs on a single plan tier. So if you're planning a setup like "managers on Enterprise, reps on Standard," confirm with Zoho or an authorized partner whether this is possible before you buy.

## Zoho CRM, Bigin or Zoho One?

Because the Zoho ecosystem is so broad, SMBs often find themselves torn between these three options.

**Bigin by Zoho CRM:** Zoho's simple, pipeline-focused CRM designed for small businesses. It is cheaper than Zoho CRM and much easier to learn, but its customization and automation capabilities are limited. It's ideal for teams of 1–5 people with simple processes. As you grow, there is a migration path to Zoho CRM.

**Zoho CRM:** The subject of this guide. It offers a flexible, customizable structure that scales with growth.

**Zoho CRM Plus and Zoho One:** CRM Plus is a bundle that combines CRM with marketing automation, customer support and social media tools. Zoho One offers dozens of applications, including accounting, HR and project management, under a single license. Zoho One can be cost-effective, especially under a model where all company employees are licensed, but if you won't actually use most of these tools, it creates unnecessary complexity.

**Decision rule:** If you're only looking for a solution for your sales team, go with Zoho CRM. If you want to bring sales, marketing and customer service under one roof, choose CRM Plus. If you're considering moving your company's entire operation to Zoho, evaluate Zoho One.

## A step-by-step decision process for choosing the right plan

Choose your plan through a structured process, not by gut feeling.

1. **Map out your sales process.** Where do leads come from, who qualifies them, what stages do they go through, who prepares the quote and who approves it? Choosing a plan without drawing this map is like tailoring a suit without taking measurements.
2. **Separate "must-haves" from "nice-to-haves."** For example, if a discount approval workflow is a must-have, Professional becomes mandatory. If advanced AI predictions are a "nice-to-have," that's not a strong enough reason to move to Enterprise.
3. **Determine your user count realistically.** Count the people who will enter data into and actively use the CRM. For managers who only need to see reports, alternative solutions (shared dashboards, scheduled report emails) can be considered.
4. **List your integrations.** Accounting, e-invoicing, website, email, phone, WhatsApp. Does each have a ready-made integration, or will you need an automation layer like n8n?
5. **Make active use of the trial period.** Zoho usually offers a 15-day trial for paid plans. Spend this time not with demo data, but by entering some of your real deals and testing with at least two sales reps.
6. **Start with a lower plan and upgrade if needed.** Upgrading plans in Zoho is easy. Rather than buying Enterprise before your needs are clear, it's often smarter to start with Professional and reassess after 6 months.
7. **Define success metrics in advance.** How will you measure whether the CRM is successful? Define metrics such as pipeline visibility, quote-to-close time, activity volume or forecast accuracy from the start. You can refer to [our B2B sales team KPI guide](/blog/b2b-sales-team-kpis) for which metrics to track.

## The most common mistakes in choosing a plan

**Getting stuck on monthly billing:** Continuing with monthly billing after the trial and keeping it that way for years means paying unnecessarily high costs. Switch to annual billing once you're confident the tool is right for you.

**Overpaying today for future growth:** Buying Enterprise for a 6-person team today because "we'll be 20 people in a year" wastes budget. Upgrading in Zoho is easy, while downgrading can be harder depending on your contract term.

**Underestimating implementation:** Zoho's flexibility turns into chaos when it's configured poorly. Forms full of unnecessary fields and illogical stages drive sales teams away from the CRM.

**Neglecting data quality:** When incomplete or incorrect data goes into the CRM, even the most expensive plan won't produce meaningful reports. When collecting personal data, also keep your KVKK (Turkish Personal Data Protection Law) obligations in mind. Review Zoho's data center options and the issue of cross-border data transfers with your legal advisor.

**Not building the CRM into a routine:** A CRM creates value when it's used regularly. Running weekly pipeline meetings directly from the CRM screen is one of the most effective ways to turn data entry into a natural habit.

## What does Zoho offer in AI and automation?

Zoho's AI assistant **Zia** offers features such as anomaly detection, best-time-to-contact suggestions, deal closure predictions and email sentiment analysis. Zia's comprehensive features are mainly available in Enterprise and higher plans. Some basic features may be available in a limited form in lower plans.

The critical question for SMBs is this: Does it make more sense to move to Enterprise for AI features, or to stay on Professional and meet this need with external tools? In many cases, the second route is more economical. For example, with n8n you can build workflows such as:

- Writing a lead from a web form into Zoho CRM, enriching the company information and generating a short qualification note with an AI model
- Sending a notification to a team channel when a deal moves to a specific stage
- Pushing closed deals to the accounting system and triggering the invoicing process

This approach lets you build an automation layer tailored to your needs while keeping licensing costs low.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Zoho CRM's free plan sufficient for SMBs?

The free plan supports up to 3 users and covers basic lead, contact and deal management. It's sufficient for very small teams or companies that want to test a CRM. However, due to its automation, reporting and record limits, a growing sales operation will soon need a paid plan.

### Can you pay for Zoho CRM in Turkish lira in Türkiye?

Zoho's regional pricing and payment options may change over time. Purchases made directly through Zoho are usually billed in foreign currency. Local partners, on the other hand, may offer different payment terms. Before you buy, it's best to confirm the current currency and invoicing options with Zoho or an authorized partner.

### When should I move from the Standard plan to Professional?

It makes sense to move to the Professional plan when your sales process requires approval workflows, mandatory stage rules, or the ability to create quotes and orders from within the CRM. If the Standard plan's automation limits are frequently falling short, that's also a strong sign it's time to upgrade.

### Is Zoho CRM cheaper than HubSpot and Pipedrive?

When comparing equivalent feature levels, Zoho CRM is generally the more economical option, especially in the mid and upper tiers. However, total cost varies depending on your setup, training and integration needs. If simplicity and fast adoption are your priorities, alternatives may offer a lower total cost despite slightly higher license fees.

## Conclusion

Zoho CRM is one of the CRM options with the strongest price-to-feature balance for SMBs in Türkiye. Still, choosing the right plan requires much more than looking at the list price. To sum up: teams of up to 3 people can start with Free or Bigin. Standard is the most sensible choice for SMBs new to CRM, while Professional is best for growing teams with defined processes that want automation. Enterprise only pays for itself in multi-team, territory-based structures. Whichever plan you choose, opt for annual billing, factor currency and VAT effects into your budget, and plan setup and training as a separate line item.

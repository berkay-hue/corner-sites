Privacy notice (KVKK)

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Text version: v1

This notice explains how your personal data is processed when you use the Draft Desk (app.socialmediacorner.co). It is provided under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”). If this English text and the Turkish text differ, the Turkish text prevails.

In short

  • Without an account, the topic you type is not stored on our servers. It is sent to an AI provider (USA) to write the drafts.
  • If you open an account, your name, email, brand voice and drafts are kept until you delete them.
  • You can download your data and delete your account in Settings.
  • Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

2. What data we process

Using the tools without an account

  • Topic and choices: The topic you type and the choices you make in the tool (platform, angle, tone and similar) are processed to write drafts. They are not stored on our servers.
  • Brand voice kept in your browser: The brand voice you set up without an account stays in your browser only. It is sent with each draft request. It is not stored on our servers.
  • Usage limit data: Your IP address and a random browser ID are used to count your daily allowance. Neither is stored in raw form on our servers. We keep only a salted, one-way summary (hash). For IPv6 addresses only the first 64 bits are used.
  • Bot check: We use Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent abuse. The check token and your IP address are sent to Cloudflare for this check.
  • Technical logs and measurement: The site runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP address and browser details to deliver and protect the service. Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, may be used to count page views in aggregate.

Waitlist and quote form

  • Waitlist: email, name, role, your explicit consent and, if you choose it, your consent to marketing emails.
  • Quote form: email, name, company, sector, how you heard about us and your language.
  • When you send these forms, your IP address and browser details (user agent) are recorded. The form content is forwarded to our team by email.

Using an account

  • Account details: full name, email, role and language. We also record when you accepted the terms. Your password is never stored in plain text. We keep only a one-way summary of it.
  • Setup and brand voice: the profile details you enter during setup, sector, tone choices, sample texts and words to avoid.
  • Draft history: the drafts you generate, with their platform, topic and creation time.
  • Session: Signing in creates a session key. Our server keeps only a summary of it.
  • Security records: IP address and email address for sign-in, sign-up, password reset and account deletion attempts. These records are kept to limit repeated attempts.
  • Sign-up notice: When you open an account, your name, email, role, language and IP address are saved as a notice record. The same details are emailed to our team.
  • Connected accounts: If you connect LinkedIn or Instagram, we process:
    • your username and account ID on that platform,
    • the access keys the platform issues (stored encrypted),
    • post and statistics data, to the extent of the permissions you grant.
    If you do not connect an account, this data is not processed.
  • Publish queue: Once publishing is available, we store:
    • the post text and media link you queue,
    • the scheduled time and approval details,
    • the result of publishing.
    No post is published without your approval.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process your personal data on the following grounds listed in Article 5 of KVKK:

  • Setting up and performing a contract (Art. 5/2-c): This ground covers:
    • writing drafts,
    • opening and running your account,
    • providing your brand voice, draft history and connected accounts,
    • replying to a quote request.
  • Complying with our legal obligations (Art. 5/2-ç): Answering lawful requests from authorities and keeping records the law requires.
  • Our legitimate interests (Art. 5/2-f): Provided this does not harm your fundamental rights, this ground covers:
    • preventing abuse and automated attacks,
    • applying the daily usage limit and keeping the service secure,
    • being notified of new sign-ups.
  • Explicit consent (Art. 5/1): Emailing you about the account version when you join the waitlist. If you ticked the marketing box, sending you promotional emails. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to info@saascorner.co. Withdrawal does not affect earlier processing.

4. How we collect data

We collect data electronically, by automated means, from these sources:

  • what you type into the tools and forms,
  • what you enter on the account screens,
  • technical details your browser sends,
  • what a LinkedIn or Instagram account you choose to connect shares with the permissions you grant.

5. Who receives your data

We share personal data with these recipients only as far as needed to run the service:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): hosting, database, bot check (Turnstile), cookie-less measurement (Web Analytics) and email routing.
  • Anthropic, PBC (USA): AI draft generation. See section 6.
  • Google LLC (Google Workspace): sending account emails such as password resets.
  • LinkedIn Corporation and Meta Platforms, Inc. (Instagram): Only if you connect your account: to set up the connection, fetch statistics within the permissions you grant and publish posts you approve.
  • Competent public authorities: Only where the law requires it.

These providers’ servers are located abroad, mainly in the USA. Your data is therefore transferred abroad. We transfer it in line with the conditions and procedures in Article 9 of KVKK.

Your data is not sold, not shared with ad networks and not used for ad targeting.

6. AI draft generation

  • When you ask for drafts, your topic, your choices and, if set, your brand voice are sent to Anthropic’s AI service. That service writes the drafts automatically.
  • Without an account, this content is not stored on our servers. With an account, the drafts and their topic are saved to your draft history.
  • Do not enter personal data in the topic field or brand voice samples. This includes other people’s names, contact details, customer information, health data or other special categories of data.
  • AI output can be wrong, incomplete or unsuitable. Read and edit a draft before you share it.
  • Draft generation only suggests text. No automated decision with legal effect is made about you.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account details, brand voice, draft history, connected accounts and publish jobs: until you delete them or delete your account. You can also delete drafts one by one.
  • Session: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Security records (IP address and email): 1 day.
  • Password reset and account connection links: deleted when they expire.
  • Usage limit summaries: 2 days. Daily totals that cannot be linked to a person may be kept indefinitely.
  • Waitlist and quote records: until your request is handled and any legal retention period ends. Deleted when you ask.
  • Sign-up notice: deleted together with your account.

When you delete your account, all records linked to it are deleted at once. Access granted to connected platforms is revoked where possible. Security records are deleted automatically within 1 day.

8. What your browser stores

This site does not set its own cookies. The items below are kept in your browser’s local storage (localStorage or sessionStorage). Clearing your browser data removes them.

  • smc-anon: a random browser ID used for the daily allowance.
  • smc-son: the last day drafts were generated.
  • smc-acik: whether you have opened the tool before.
  • smc-tema, smc-dil: your theme and language.
  • smc-ses-v1: your brand voice without an account, in this browser only.
  • smc-konu: keeps your topic if the page reloads. Removed when the tab closes.
  • smc-hesap-not: a short message for the account screens. Removed when the tab closes.
  • smc-oturum: your session key. Kept until you sign out if you chose “Remember me”, otherwise until the tab closes.

9. Your rights under KVKK

Under Article 11 of KVKK you may apply to us to:

  1. Learn whether your personal data is processed.
  2. Request information about the processing if it is.
  3. Learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose.
  4. Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred.
  5. Ask for incomplete or inaccurate data to be corrected.
  6. Ask for your data to be erased or destroyed under the conditions in Article 7 of KVKK.
  7. Ask for corrections, erasure or destruction to be notified to third parties who received your data.
  8. Object to an outcome against you that results solely from automated analysis of your data.
  9. Claim compensation if you suffer damage because your data was processed unlawfully.

10. How to make a request

  • If you have an account: On the Settings page you can do two things right away:
    • use “Download my data” to download your account data as one file,
    • use “Delete account” to delete your account and all records linked to it.
  • By email: Write to info@saascorner.co. Where possible, use the email address from your account or form so we can verify your identity.
  • In writing: Send a letter to the address above, or use registered electronic mail (KEP). Your request should include:
    • your full name,
    • your Turkish ID number, or for non-citizens your nationality and passport or ID number,
    • your postal address,
    • your email address and phone number, if any,
    • your request.

We answer requests within 30 days at the latest. As a rule, no charge applies. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

11. Changes to this notice

If the way we process data changes, we update this notice and change the date and version at the top. If a change is significant, we also tell account holders directly. For the account terms, see the Terms page.

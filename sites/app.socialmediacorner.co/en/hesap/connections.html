Connections

Connect your LinkedIn and Instagram accounts; drafts you schedule in the publishing queue are shared to them automatically on the day and time you pick. Auto-sharing is on the Pro plan; you can remove a connection at any time.

Which permissions do we ask for?
  • Profile or page name and photo
  • Post metrics (read only)
  • Posting permission: requested separately once auto-sharing opens; only drafts you schedule are shared
  • No messaging permission is requested

Your website (blog)

Connect your site and a blog post is written for you and sent to it on the days and time you pick. Posts arrive as drafts by default, so you review and publish them. Your key is stored encrypted, and you can remove the connection at any time.

About Brand Corner

Brand Corner is an influencer marketing platform connecting brands, influencers, and agencies worldwide. Built in Istanbul as part of Corner Group.

What is Brand Corner?

Brand Corner is an influencer marketing platform that brings brands, influencers, and agencies together in one place. Brands use it to find and work with influencers who fit their brand, influencers use it to get discovered and manage collaborations, and agencies use it to manage their talent roster and client campaigns.

Who it is for

  • Brands

    Discover, connect, and collaborate with influencers who align with your brand values.

  • Influencers

    Get discovered by brands and manage your collaborations in one place.

  • Agencies

    Manage your talent roster and streamline client campaigns.

Where we are

Brand Corner is built in Istanbul, Türkiye, and works with brands, influencers, and agencies worldwide.

Who is behind Brand Corner

Brand Corner was founded by Berkay Yalçın, a B2B sales consultant with 10+ years of experience. You can find him on LinkedIn and at yalcinberkay.com.tr.

Part of Corner Group

Brand Corner is part of Corner Group, alongside SAAS Corner, Sales Corner, and Social Media Corner.

2. In short

  • The website has no user accounts, no sign-in and no online payments.
  • The only form on the website is the newsletter sign-up in the footer of some pages. It collects your email address, and our form service also records your IP address and browser information.
  • We do not set cookies ourselves and do not use advertising or analytics tracking scripts.
  • Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the website.
  • The About page and the blog load fonts from Google Fonts, and the blog also shows images hosted by Pexels; these providers receive your IP address.

3. What we collect

We collect personal data electronically, in two ways: through the form you fill in, and automatically when your browser loads the website.

Newsletter form

The home, features, pricing and solutions pages have a "Stay Updated" newsletter form in the footer. When you submit it, your browser sends your entry to our form service, which runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores each submission in a Cloudflare D1 database. A submission contains:

  • the email address you enter;
  • the page you submitted it from and the website name (brandcorner.co);
  • the date and time of the submission;
  • your IP address; and
  • your browser's user-agent string (information about your browser and operating system, up to 300 characters).

Giving your email address is voluntary and is not a legal or contractual requirement. If you do not give it, you will not receive our news and updates; you can still use the rest of the website.

A copy of the submission (your email address, the page, the website name and the time, but not your IP address or user-agent string) is then sent by email to our team's mailbox.

To prevent spam and abuse, we limit how many submissions can be sent from one IP address in a short period.

When you visit the website

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver pages to you and protect the service, Cloudflare processes technical data about each request, such as your IP address, the address requested, the time of the request and your browser's user-agent string. How Cloudflare handles this data is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare also asks browsers that support it to report network errors (Network Error Logging). If a request to the website fails, your browser may send Cloudflare a short report with technical details of the failed request, such as the address requested and the type of error. Successful requests are not reported.

Fonts and images from other providers

The About page, the blog and blog posts load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), and the blog and blog posts show images hosted by Pexels (images.pexels.com). When you open these pages, your browser connects directly to Google and Pexels, which receive your IP address and browser information. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Pexels Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

Because the website has no accounts, sign-in or online payments, we do not collect passwords or payment details through it. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking scripts and do not build profiles of visitors.

  • Email address used to send you news and updates: 2 years from your last contact with us, or until you object.
  • Records of your consent and objection (sign-up email, page, website name, date and time): for the period required by law.

8. Security

The website is served over HTTPS and tells browsers to connect to it only over HTTPS (HSTS). The form service only accepts new submissions; it offers no way to read stored submissions over the internet. No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure.

9. Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Brand Corner itself does not set any cookies. See "Cloudflare" below for technical cookies our hosting provider may set.

Language preference

The home, features, pricing and solutions pages have a TR/EN language switch. When you use it, your choice ("en" or "tr") is saved in your browser's local storage under the name brand-corner-lang, so the page can show the same language next time. It contains nothing else, is not sent to us, and stays in your browser until you clear the site's data in your browser. Because it is saved only when you use the switch, and only to remember your choice, the website does not show a cookie consent banner.

Vercel Web Analytics (inactive)

The home, features, pricing and solutions pages, and the page shown when an address is not found, still include a reference to Vercel Web Analytics from the site's original build. On our current hosting, that reference loads an empty file from our own domain, so no analytics code runs and no analytics data is collected or sent.

Cloudflare

10. Your rights under the GDPR and KVKK

Depending on where you live and which law applies, you have the rights below. To use them, contact us at info@saascorner.co. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on a request.

Under the GDPR

  • Access: get a copy of your personal data and information about how we use it (Article 15).
  • Rectification: have inaccurate data corrected (Article 16).
  • Erasure: have your data deleted in certain cases (Article 17).
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data in certain cases (Article 18).
  • Data portability: receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format, where processing is based on consent or a contract and is carried out by automated means (Article 20).
  • Objection: object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and object at any time to the use of your data for direct marketing (Article 21).
  • Withdrawal of consent: where processing is based on your consent, withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal (Article 7(3)).
  • Complaint: complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place (Article 77). See the list of EU supervisory authorities.

We respond within one month of receiving your request. Where necessary, taking into account the complexity and number of requests, this period can be extended by two further months; if so, we will tell you within the first month (Article 12(3)).

Under KVKK (Law No. 6698, Article 11)

You have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed;
  • request information about the processing if your data has been processed;
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether your data is used in line with that purpose;
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred;
  • ask for your data to be corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate;
  • ask for your data to be erased or destroyed under the conditions in Article 7 of KVKK;
  • ask for the third parties to whom your data was transferred to be notified of a correction, erasure or destruction;
  • object to a result against you that arises from the analysis of your data exclusively by automated systems; and
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because your data was processed unlawfully.

We conclude KVKK applications as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest, free of charge, unless the action requires an additional cost, in which case a fee set by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged (Article 13). If your application is rejected, you find our answer insufficient or we do not answer in time, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu, kvkk.gov.tr) within 30 days of learning our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your application (Article 14).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

Online payments are not accepted at the moment.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No long-term contracts required. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Yearly plans are billed annually but can be cancelled with remaining time prorated.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes influencer browsing, 3 campaigns per month, basic analytics and email support.

How much does the Professional plan cost?

The Professional plan is $99 per month. It adds unlimited campaigns, advanced analytics, priority support, team collaboration for 3 users and custom reports.

What does the Enterprise plan include?

Enterprise has custom pricing. It includes everything in Professional plus unlimited users, a dedicated account manager, custom integrations and white-label options. Contact sales for a quote.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. You can start on the free plan without a credit card.

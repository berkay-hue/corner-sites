This notice explains how the personal data you send us through socialmediacorner.co is processed under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698).

Data we process

The name, email address, company details and message you share in the contact form or by email.

Purpose and legal basis

We use your data only to reply to your request, prepare a quote and stay in touch with you. The legal basis is steps prior to entering a contract and our legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-c and 5/2-f).

Sharing

We never sell your data. It may be shared only with the providers that host this site and deliver email, limited to that purpose; some of them may be located abroad.

Retention

It is kept for as long as needed to answer your request and for the retention periods set by law; it is then deleted or anonymised.

Cookies

This section is the Cookie Policy of socialmediacorner.co. Our site uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We do not use third-party tools that measure your visit or recognise you on other sites (such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel). Fonts and images are served from our own server too.

The site stores only this one entry in your browser:

  • smc-ck: Remembers the choice you made in the cookie banner (all means “I accept”, ess means “Essential only”), so the banner does not appear again on every page. This entry is not a cookie: it is kept in your browser’s local storage (localStorage). It is never sent to our server and stays until you delete it.

The site itself sets no cookies. Our hosting provider Cloudflare may add a short-lived, strictly necessary cookie (__cf_bm or cf_clearance) for a security check when it detects unusual traffic. It is used only to protect the site against abuse.

Because there are no optional cookies today, the option you pick in the banner does not change how the site works. When you follow external links such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Cal.com or Calendly, those sites apply their own cookie policies. Our app at app.socialmediacorner.co has its own privacy notice.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie preferences” button in the footer of every page. To remove the entry completely, clear this site’s data in your browser. The banner will appear again on your next visit.

Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you can ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, object, and claim compensation for damage. Send requests to info@saascorner.co.

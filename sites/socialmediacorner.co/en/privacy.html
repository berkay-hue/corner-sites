Privacy Notice

This notice explains how the personal data you send us through socialmediacorner.co is processed under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698).

Data controller

Data we process

The name, email address, company details and message you share in the contact form or by email.

When you open our blog pages (the blog index and posts), the stock photos there load from Pexels, so your IP address and browser details (such as browser type and language preference) reach Pexels.

Purpose and legal basis

We use the data you share in the contact form or by email to reply to your request, prepare a quote and stay in touch with you. The legal basis is steps prior to entering a contract and our legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-c and 5/2-f).

The IP address and browser details that reach Pexels on our blog pages are processed so that we can show our posts with their images. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-f).

Sharing

We never sell your data. It is shared with these recipients, limited to the stated purpose:

  • Hosting and email providers: To publish this site and deliver email. Some of them may be located abroad.
  • Pexels GmbH (Germany): To load the stock photos on our blog pages (the blog index and posts). When you open these pages, your browser sends your IP address and browser details directly to Pexels. Pexels processes them under its own privacy policy.

Transfers to recipients abroad are made in line with the conditions and procedures in Article 9 of KVKK.

Retention

It is kept for as long as needed to answer your request and for the retention periods set by law; it is then deleted or anonymised.

Cookies

This section is the Cookie Policy of socialmediacorner.co. Our site uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We do not use third-party tools that measure your visit or recognise you on other sites (such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel). Fonts are served from our own server; stock photos on our blog pages (the blog index and posts) come from Pexels.

The site stores only this one entry in your browser:

  • smc-ck: Remembers that you closed the cookie notice with “Got it” (value ess; an all value left over from the banner’s earlier version means the same), so the banner does not appear again on every page. This entry is not a cookie: it is kept in your browser’s local storage (localStorage). It is never sent to our server and stays until you delete it.

The site itself sets no cookies. Our hosting provider Cloudflare may add a short-lived, strictly necessary cookie (__cf_bm or cf_clearance) for a security check when it detects unusual traffic. It is used only to protect the site against abuse. When photos on our blog pages load, Pexels’ infrastructure may also set short-lived security cookies (__cf_bm, _cfuvid) on the pexels.com domain; these cookies are not sent to us.

Because there are no optional cookies, the banner does not ask for consent but only informs you; the “Got it” button closes it and turns nothing else on. When you follow external links such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Cal.com or Calendly, those sites apply their own cookie policies. Our app at app.socialmediacorner.co has its own privacy notice.

Seeing the notice again

You can open the banner again at any time with the “Cookie notice” button in the footer of every page. To remove the entry completely, clear this site’s data in your browser. The banner will appear again on your next visit.

Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you can ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, object, and claim compensation for damage. Send requests to info@saascorner.co.

How is a quote prepared?

  1. Intro call: We review your goals, your current accounts and any materials you've prepared.
  2. Scope: Together we pin down which accounts we manage, how often we post and how reporting works.
  3. Quote: We send you a written quote tailored to that scope.

What shapes the quote?

  • How many company and personal accounts we manage
  • Platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram and others)
  • Monthly volume of posts, articles and blog posts
  • Need for competitor analysis, A/B testing and reporting

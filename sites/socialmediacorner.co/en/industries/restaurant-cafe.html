Social media management for restaurants and cafés

Social Media Corner manages restaurant and café social media with Reels and Stories that show the menu, the kitchen and the atmosphere, a regular publishing schedule and reporting. The goal is that guests get to know your venue, and remember it, before they walk in.

For a restaurant or café, social media is where guests experience the place before they walk in. People often decide where to go from a dish they saw on Instagram, a Reel or a friend's Story.

Why social media matters for restaurants and cafés

In food and drink, decisions are quick and largely visual. When menu updates, seasonal items, events and everyday moments are shared regularly, new guests come in and regulars feel closer to the brand. A consistent brand identity makes the venue easier to remember and recommend.

What we do for restaurants and cafés

  • Instagram Reels and Stories: Short videos and Stories that show the menu, the kitchen and the atmosphere.
  • Design and video support: Design support and video editing keep every post looking its best.
  • Hashtags and Explore visibility: Effective hashtags help your content reach the right people.
  • Influencer and partnership strategy: We plan collaborations that suit your venue and bring in new guests.
  • Targeting and ads: We define your audience and use paid ads and campaigns to promote openings, events and new menus.

How we work

We start with a call to learn about your venue and goals. Then we build a content plan that fits your brand and set a publishing schedule. Performance is tracked through reporting and analytics, and competitor and trend analysis keeps the plan current. AI-assisted content planning helps keep posting regular and consistent.

Instead of a fixed price list, we quote based on your needs; posting frequency, video needs and campaign goals set the scope.

FAQ

Common questions

More questions? Email us: info@socialmediacorner.co

What is Social Media Corner?

Social Media Corner is a Corner Group social media management service that runs Instagram and LinkedIn for brands. Strategy, Reels and Stories, LinkedIn content, blog writing, targeting and reporting are handled by one team. It was founded by Berkay Yalçın, who brings 10+ years of B2B sales experience. Every engagement starts with a call to understand your needs; scope and price depend on the package and your goals.

What is social media management?

Social media management means running a brand's social accounts on an ongoing basis: strategy, content production, publishing schedule, targeting, ads and reporting. The goal is to grow awareness and engagement against measurable targets.

Who is Social Media Corner for?

Businesses that want their social media handled consistently and professionally. The Starter Package covers core content and reporting, the Professional Package adds advanced strategy, and Startup Friendly is for brands that want a dedicated account manager and multi-platform distribution. We also work with executives who want to build a personal LinkedIn presence from scratch.

How does pricing work?

There is no fixed price list; we quote based on your needs. Pick the Starter, Professional or Startup Friendly package, request a quote, and we'll tailor it to the platforms, content volume and goals involved.

Which platforms do you manage?

We focus on Instagram and LinkedIn: Reels and Stories on Instagram, and content and articles for company and personal accounts on LinkedIn. SEO-optimized blog posts support both, and the Startup Friendly package adds multi-platform distribution.

How does reporting work?

Reporting and analytics are included in every package. We track campaigns with real-time reports and live monitoring, and in the Professional Package we optimize content with A/B tests and competitor analysis. In Startup Friendly, the plan is reviewed every quarter.

What's included in the packages?

Packages include blog writing, Instagram Reels and Stories, LinkedIn content and articles, strategic planning, targeting, revisions and reporting. The top package adds influencer and partnership strategy, video editing, paid ads and campaign setup, and a dedicated account manager.

How does the ROI play out?

Social media pays off most over the long term, and the key is consistency. ROI isn't just a short-term metric; it reflects a sustained, strategic investment. Continuous optimization and adapting to market changes are what grow the return over time.

How do you handle different cultures and languages?

With AI support and an international setup, we can produce content in several languages for different cultural audiences. Our main strength is English content: engaging, high-quality material built for a broad audience. We balance global reach with local cultural nuance.

How do we get started?

Our team comes from a sales background, so we focus on content that actually moves sales. We usually start with a meeting where we listen to your needs and review the materials you've prepared. That lets us shape the strategy around your sales performance and deliver targeted, effective content.

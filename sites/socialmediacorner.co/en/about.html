About Social Media Corner

Social Media Corner is a social media management brand that runs companies' social accounts end to end, from strategy and content to reporting. It is part of Corner Group.

What makes us different?

Our team comes from sales. That's why we build content to support your sales process, not just to collect likes. Alongside company pages, we also grow founders' and executives' personal LinkedIn profiles.

Founder

Social Media Corner was founded by Berkay Yalçın, founder of the Corner Group companies, who has 10+ years of experience in sales and marketing.

Which industries do we work with?

  • SaaS and B2B tech
  • E-commerce
  • Restaurants and cafes

How can I contact Social Media Corner?

Fill in the form on this page or email info@socialmediacorner.co. You can write to us in Turkish or English.

What happens after I get in touch?

We usually start with a meeting where we listen to your needs and review the materials you've prepared. The strategy is shaped around what we discuss in that meeting.

How do I get a price quote?

We don't have a fixed price list; we quote based on your needs. Tell us which of the Starter, Professional or Startup Friendly packages fits you, and we prepare a custom quote based on the platforms to manage, content volume and your goals.

What can I contact you about?

Write to us about managing your Instagram and LinkedIn accounts, our packages, tailored plans or technical questions about our service.

How does the ROI play out?

At the start we agree on the goal (awareness, website traffic or quote requests) and the metrics we will track: reach and impressions, engagement rate, profile and page visits, website visits from UTM-tagged links, form fills and quote requests. We share these every month in a report that compares them with the previous month, and we adjust the next month's content plan based on the results. On social media, return usually builds up over a few months of consistent work.

How do you handle different cultures and languages?

With AI support and an international setup, we can produce content in several languages for different cultural audiences. Our main strength is English content: engaging, high-quality material built for a broad audience. We balance global reach with local cultural nuance.

How do we get started?

Our team comes from a sales background, so we focus on content that actually moves sales. We usually start with a meeting where we listen to your needs and review the materials you've prepared. That lets us shape the strategy around your sales performance and deliver targeted, effective content.

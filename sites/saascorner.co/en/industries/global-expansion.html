The first challenge in a new market: who to reach, and how

For a B2B company that has established its product or service in Türkiye, expanding into global markets is a natural next step. But in a new market it's often unclear which industries, company sizes and decision-maker roles to target. Without a local network and brand awareness, landing the first sales meetings can be harder than building the product.

At SAAS Corner, we support companies going global with strategic planning, market analysis and tailored sales tools. On your behalf, we research the target market, identify the companies and people that match your ideal customer profile, run outbound campaigns and put qualified sales meetings straight on your calendar.

How we work for global growth

  • Discovery meetings: We review your product, target markets, current customers and sales cycle together.
  • Market research: We define target industries, company sizes and decision-maker roles in the new market, then build campaign lists.
  • Outbound sales strategy: We design multichannel campaigns combining email sequences, LinkedIn outreach and cold calling.
  • Campaign optimization: We continuously refine messaging, segments and channels based on reply and meeting data.

These steps are part of our eight-stage system: discovery, market research, custom strategy, training, outbound, campaign optimization, feedback and results. Data from each stage feeds the next, so your sales effort in the new market is based on observation, not guesswork.

Who is it for?

This service suits B2B companies that want to sell abroad from Türkiye, test demand in a new market before hiring a local team, or make their global pipeline more consistent. You start working with an experienced team without the cost of building your own sales department.

As the first meetings come in from the new market, we review together which segments and messages resonate. Once the market is clear and you want your own team, we can plan that transition with Sales Team Building and Sales Consulting. Global growth stops being a one-off experiment and becomes a measurable, repeatable process.

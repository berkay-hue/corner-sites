Frequently asked questions

How can I contact Sales Corner?

Fill in the form on this page or email us at info@saascorner.co. Our office is at Mehmet Ali Paşa Mah. Ogün Sok. No:1, Kocaeli.

What happens after I get in touch?

We start with a free 20-minute discovery call. You leave the call with a custom workflow blueprint built for your business.

What do we cover in the discovery call?

We listen to your repetitive sales processes and design an n8n + AI architecture tailored to you. Before the call, you can browse the example workflows on our home page to see which processes can be automated.

What can you help with?

Our main focus is HubSpot integration, and our second is AI automation: we automate processes such as email automation, post-meeting follow-ups, lead generation and CRM sync with AI workflows. The other subjects on the form are booking sales meetings (8+), expanding into a new country, sales consultancy and LinkedIn consultancy.

Frequently asked questions

What does Sales Corner do?

Sales Corner automates B2B sales processes with AI and n8n. We hand repetitive sales work — from lead generation to sales analytics — to AI workflows so your team can focus on selling.

Which sales processes can you automate?

We automate lead generation, CRM sync, email automation, WhatsApp bots, pre-meeting briefs, sales call analysis, competitor analysis and proposal writing with AI workflows.

What ready-made automations do you have?

We run 15+ active AI workflows, including Live Sales Coach, Conversation IQ, Sequence Forge, Briefing Studio, Competitor Radar and Apollo Pipeline. Each one owns a specific sales step end to end.

How does an AI workflow work?

Every workflow follows a 5-layer architecture: Trigger, Data Enrichment, AI Layer, Action and Logging. An event starts the workflow, the data is enriched, AI makes the call, the action runs and every step is logged.

Which tools and technologies do you use?

We use n8n for orchestration and connect tools such as Apollo.io, Gemini, Claude, AssemblyAI, Fireflies, Twilio, ElevenLabs, Google Sheets, Drive, Gmail, LinkedIn API, Airtable and Supabase in a single flow.

How fast and how often do the automations run?

Workflows run 24/7 with an average trigger time of 7 seconds.

We start with a free 20-minute discovery call, and you leave with a custom workflow blueprint for your business. You can book the call from our contact page. Prefer to try the tools yourself? Start right away from the dashboard with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

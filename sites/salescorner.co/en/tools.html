Which tool should you use when?

  • Before sending a campaign: use the subject line scorer to catch what hurts open rates and the cold email checker to catch what hurts reply rates.
  • When planning a quarter or month: the sales target calculator turns your revenue target into the number of leads, meetings and proposals you need.
  • When discussing a tool or automation budget: the time and ROI calculator shows the hours your team spends on repetitive work and the return on automating it.
  • When building a list: the LinkedIn Boolean search builder prepares a query that finds people with the right titles in the right industries.
  • When starting cold email on a new domain: the domain warm-up planner gives you a schedule for ramping up daily sending volume step by step.
  • When qualifying a deal: the BANT / MEDDIC scorecard shows how qualified the deal is and what information is missing.
  • When discussing comp plans and unit economics: the commission calculator works out a rep's earnings and the CAC / LTV calculator shows whether customer acquisition cost pays off.

How do they work?

The tools run on transparent rules and formulas; they don't use AI or any external service. Every page explains exactly how the score or result is calculated, so you can walk your team through the reasoning. The text and numbers you enter never leave your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. All nine tools are free; they don't ask for a sign-up, an email address or a credit card.

Is the data I enter sent anywhere?

No. All calculations and scoring are done with JavaScript running in your browser. The subject lines, email copy and numbers you type are not sent to a server and are not stored.

Do the tools use AI?

No. These tools run on transparent rules and formulas; every page explains exactly how the score or result is calculated. To prepare personalized emails, competitor analyses and meeting briefings with AI, see the Sales Corner dashboard.

How do I share results with my team?

Results stay in your browser. You can take a screenshot or ask a teammate to enter the same values in their own tool; values are not added to the link, so it never contains personal data.

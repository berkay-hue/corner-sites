2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a site saves in your browser. localStorage stores information in your browser in a similar way. In this policy we call both “cookies”.

  • Essential: needed for the site and panel to work, stay secure and remember your choice. They cannot be switched off.
  • Advertising and measurement: used to measure whether our ads work. They run only if you click “Accept”.

We do not use any other analytics, tracking or targeting cookies on the site.

4. Legal bases

  • Essential cookies: necessary to provide the service (KVKK Art. 5/2-c) and our legitimate interest in security (Art. 5/2-f).
  • Advertising and measurement: your explicit consent (Art. 5/1). Google’s servers are outside Türkiye; the transfer follows KVKK Article 9.

5. Managing your choice

Use Change my cookie preferences to reopen the banner and give or withdraw consent at any time. The “Cookie settings” link in every page footer does the same. You can also delete or block cookies and site data in your browser; blocking essential cookies may stop forms and the panel from working. For Google ad preferences see My Ad Center.

6. Fonts and external links

Blog pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which sends your IP address and browser data to Google; no cookie is set. LinkedIn, YouTube and other social links only open those sites when you click them; nothing is sent to them before that.

7. Your rights

To use your rights under KVKK Article 11, write to info@saascorner.co. Turkish speakers can also use the KVKK application form.

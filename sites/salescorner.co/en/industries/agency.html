Sales Corner for agencies

At agencies, new business usually comes from proposals the busiest account manager writes in the evening. Sales Corner automates analysing a prospect’s competitors and website, preparing for the first meeting, turning the discovery call into a brief and tracking competitors on the client’s behalf. Your team focuses on the strategic part of the proposal.

14-day free trial·No credit card requiredSee plans and pricing

Typical problems for agency sales teams

  • Researching a prospect’s competitors and digital presence from scratch for every proposal takes days.
  • The agency’s own new business development is the first thing to stop when client work gets busy.
  • What was discussed in the discovery meeting reaches the brief incomplete; the proposal misses the client’s real problem.
  • Reporting regularly on what an existing client’s competitors are doing creates extra work, and when it is skipped, the relationship weakens.
  • Proposal decks need to carry the agency’s visual identity.

Which module, and how?

Competitor Analysis: the backbone of the proposal

Scans the prospect’s competitor websites, compares strengths and weaknesses and produces a ready-made deck. The "situation analysis" section of your proposal arrives as a draft within minutes; your team adds the interpretation and strategy.

See Competitor Analysis in Automations

SEO Analysis: a concrete audit attached to the proposal

Gives an SEO score, gaps, keyword volumes and a content suggestion for the prospect’s website. The answer to "what will we fix" in the proposal is backed by numbers and examples.

See SEO Analysis in Automations

Meeting Brief: walk into the first meeting prepared

Researches the company and contacts, scores the opportunity and prepares a briefing deck. Before the meeting you decide who joins the pitch and which case study to highlight.

See Meeting Brief in Automations

Sequence Writer: the agency’s own new business flow

Writes a cold call script and LinkedIn message sequence tailored to the target company. New business development keeps going even in busy periods; a separate, personalised sequence is ready for each target.

See Sequence Writer in Automations

Meeting Analysis (Fireflies): from discovery call to brief

Scores Fireflies recordings through a sales coach’s eyes and extracts objections, BANT signals and a follow-up email. The budget and timing discussed in the discovery call reach the proposal team in full.

Explore meeting analysis

Competitor Tracking: the weekly report that keeps clients

Monitors your client’s competitors’ blog, RSS and LinkedIn posts every week; a summary and a recommended action arrive in your inbox. When you add this summary to your monthly report, the agency is no longer just the side that does the work but the side that watches the market.

See Competitor Tracking in Automations

Sample workflow

  1. Target list: For companies in the industry you want to work in, prepare a LinkedIn sequence and a call script with Sequence Writer.
  2. Before the first meeting: Get to know the company and the attendees with Meeting Brief; choose the pitch team based on the opportunity score.
  3. Discovery call: Feed the Fireflies recording to Meeting Analysis; move the resulting needs, budget and timing notes into the proposal brief.
  4. Proposal: Put the Competitor Analysis deck and the SEO Analysis findings into the situation analysis section of the proposal; your team writes the strategy.
  5. Once they become a client: Add the client’s competitors to Competitor Tracking; work the weekly summaries into the monthly report.

Frequently asked questions

How does Sales Corner speed up an agency’s proposal process?

Competitor Analysis scans the prospect’s competitors and produces a comparative deck, and SEO Analysis lists the site’s gaps and content suggestions. The situation analysis section of the proposal arrives as a draft; the agency team focuses on strategy and the creative work.

Can I extract discovery meeting notes automatically?

Yes. Meeting Analysis takes the transcript of your Fireflies recordings and extracts objections, BANT signals and a follow-up email. You can move these notes straight into the proposal brief.

Can we put our agency’s logo on the decks?

On the Professional, Team and Custom AI plans, your own logo appears on the decks. Current plan details are listed in the pricing section.

Can we track our clients’ competitors regularly?

Competitor Tracking monitors the blog, RSS and LinkedIn posts of the competitors you add every week and sends a summary with recommended actions to your inbox. You can add these summaries to your client reports.

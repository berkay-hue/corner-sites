    How is it calculated?

    For each task, the weekly hours per rep are multiplied by the share of that task handed to automation; the result is multiplied by 4.33 weeks a month and by the number of reps to get the hours your team wins back each month. Those hours are valued at the hourly cost you entered. Net gain = value of time − plan fee; ROI = net gain ÷ plan fee.

    With the default values (1 rep, 6 + 5 + 3 hours a week, 50% / 60% / 50% automation), the rep wins back 7.5 hours a week, about 32.5 hours a month, which is more than four working days. For a rep with an hourly cost of ₺450, that time is worth about ₺14,600; after the Professional plan's ₺3,490 monthly fee, the net gain is about ₺11,100 and the ROI about 320%.

    How do I find the hourly cost?

    Divide the rep's total monthly cost (gross salary, employer contributions, commission and benefits) by monthly working hours (about 170 hours). For example, a total monthly cost of ₺75,000 comes to about ₺440 an hour. Because Sales Corner plans are priced in Turkish lira, enter the cost in lira too so the ROI compares like with like. Hours won back are not cash on their own; their value comes from the rep spending that time on more conversations and follow-ups.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you calculate the ROI of sales automation?

    Multiply the hours won back through automation by the rep's hourly cost, subtract the tool's fee and divide the remainder by the fee. For example, a ₺3,490 plan that saves ₺14,600 worth of time a month has an ROI of about 320%.

    Where do the automation shares come from?

    50% for research, 60% for email writing and 50% for meeting prep are default estimates: automation prepares the draft, while review and the final touch stay with the rep. You can change them in the "Edit automation assumptions" section based on your own experience.

    Which plan should I choose?

    Starter suits a single user with low volume; Professional suits reps who prepare many sequences and briefs a week and small teams of up to 3; Team suits teams of 4–5. For more than 5 users, get in touch with us.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes. Every panel plan starts with a 14-day free trial and no credit card is required.

    Are the values I enter saved?

    No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser; the values are not sent to a server and no sign-up is required.

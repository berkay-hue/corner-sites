What we collect

  • Data you give us: name, work email, phone, company, job title and your message in the contact form.
  • In the panel: your sign-in email, plan and usage records, what you enter into the tools and the results; if you connect Fireflies, your API key and meeting transcripts. You sign in with an email link, not a password.
  • Automatically: IP address, browser and device data, pages visited, referrer and cookie identifiers.
  • Public sources: business contact and job-title data from public company websites and professional networks, for business outreach.

We do not collect card data on this site.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and run meetings and proposals
  • To run your panel account, the tools, and plan and usage limits
  • To present our services
  • To keep the site working and secure
  • To measure our ads (only with your cookie consent)
  • To meet legal obligations

Filling in the form is not consent to marketing email; we send marketing only with separate consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Cookies

Non-essential cookies run only with your consent: the Google Ads tag loads only if you click “Accept” in the cookie banner. Change your choice with the Cookie settings link in every footer or in your browser. The full list is in our Cookie Policy. Blog pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which sends your IP address to Google.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only as needed with hosting, database, email, AI and ad-measurement providers (mainly Cloudflare, Supabase in the EU/Frankfurt, Anthropic, Google, Serper, ScrapingBee, and Fireflies if you connect it), our advisers, and authorities where the law requires. Some providers are outside Türkiye; transfers follow KVKK Article 9.

Security

We use encryption in transit, role-based access, least privilege and backups. If a breach occurs we notify the people affected and the Turkish Data Protection Authority within the legal time limit.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for its purpose and for the periods the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask whether we process your data, get information, request correction or deletion, object to automated decisions and claim damages (KVKK Article 11). Write to info@saascorner.co; we reply within 30 days.

Children

The site is for businesses; we do not knowingly collect data from people under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always on this page.

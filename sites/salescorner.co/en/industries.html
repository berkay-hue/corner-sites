Sales Corner by industry

Same modules, different sales cycles. In SaaS the demo takes centre stage, in e-commerce it is short, seasonal calls, and at an agency it is the proposal. On the pages below you will find each industry’s sales problems, which module is used and how, a sample workflow and a representative output.

Preparing for decision-makers in a long sales cycle, discovery call analysis and pre-proposal research.

Sales Corner for software teams selling abroad

English outbound sequences, comparison pages against global competitors and international demo preparation.

Which modules are there?

  • Meeting Brief: company and contact research before the meeting, an opportunity score and a briefing deck.
  • Sequence Writer: a cold call script and LinkedIn message sequence tailored to the target company.
  • Competitor Analysis and Competitor Tracking: a comparison of competitor websites plus weekly blog, RSS and LinkedIn monitoring.
  • SEO Analysis: SEO score, gaps, keyword volumes and a content suggestion.
  • Meeting Analysis (Fireflies): call recordings scored through a sales coach’s eyes, objections, BANT and a follow-up email.

Frequently asked questions

Which industries is Sales Corner a fit for?

Sales Corner is designed for every team that sells B2B. In this section we walk through, module by module, the seven industries where it is used most: SaaS, e-commerce, agencies, manufacturing, logistics, consulting and software teams selling abroad.

Are the sample outputs on the industry pages real?

No. The sample outputs are representative texts written by hand for fictional companies; they show the structure of what the modules produce and contain no real customer data.

What if my industry is not on the list?

The modules work independently of industry; you can try them on your own target accounts during the 14-day free trial. If you need a custom setup, write to us from the contact page.

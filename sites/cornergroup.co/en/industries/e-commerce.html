Problem

What makes e-commerce growth hard

E-commerce brands usually work with data scattered across ads, social media, marketplaces and their own store. It isn't clear which campaign actually drives sales, which customers buy again and which segments are being lost.

The problem grows when you manage multiple brands or sales channels. When social content, ad campaigns and customer communication are run by different teams, messaging loses consistency and budget is spent on channels you can't measure.

Solution

Which Corners come into play?

For e-commerce brands, we tie content and ad management and customer data to the same goal. These Corners usually come into play:

Social Media Corner ↗

Content calendar, video production, Meta and TikTok ad management, and monthly reporting. It keeps your brand's voice on social media consistent.

CRM Corner ↗

A Teknopark-approved, locally built CRM. It brings customer and sales data into a single flow, laying the foundation for segmentation and retention work.

Sales Corner ↗

Automates cross-channel data transfer and repetitive marketing tasks with n8n and AI.

Process

How do we work?

First we review your current sales channels, ad accounts and where your customer data lives. Then a content and ad plan is prepared, customer data is unified in the CRM and repetitive tasks are automated.

Social media and ad performance are reported every month and campaigns are optimized monthly. The goal is a transparent setup where you can see what each channel delivers and allocate budget accordingly. Scope and timeline are planned around your brand's needs.

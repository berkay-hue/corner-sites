Problem

Where SaaS sales get stuck

Many SaaS companies focus on building the product and leave their pipeline to the founders' personal networks or irregular inbound requests. That may bring in the first customers, but growth eventually slows: which segment closes faster, which message gets replies and what the pipeline will deliver next quarter all remain unclear.

In a subscription model the job doesn't end with the sale. Activating trial users, speeding up onboarding and retaining customers are all part of the sales process. When these steps are spread across different tools, data gets fragmented and the team ends up tracking the same customer on different screens.

Solution

Which Corners come into play?

SaaS companies usually bring in three Corners together. We decide which combination suits you in the discovery call; you can also use the brands individually.

SAAS Corner ↗

Our outbound sales engine, serving more than 100 partners worldwide. It builds target lists and personalized email and LinkedIn sequences based on your ideal customer profile.

Sales Corner ↗

Sales automation with n8n and AI: it automates repetitive work such as lead enrichment, follow-up reminders and onboarding steps.

CRM Corner ↗

A Teknopark-approved, locally built CRM integrated with Apollo, LinkedIn and Gmail. It collects every opportunity from outbound into a single pipeline.

Process

How do we work?

First we review your ideal customer profile and existing sales data together. Then the selected Corners start working as one team: SAAS Corner prepares the target list and messaging, CRM Corner sets up the pipeline and Sales Corner builds the automations.

Results are reported every week and campaigns are optimized monthly. In a typical engagement the infrastructure is set up in the first three months, the system settles in by month six and scaling begins by the end of the first year. Timelines depend on your team size and existing infrastructure.

FAQ

Questions from SaaS companies

As an early-stage SaaS startup, when should we start outbound sales?
You can start testing outbound as soon as you can clearly define whose problem your product solves. In the discovery call we review your ideal customer profile together and, SAAS Corner in hand, propose a small-scale starter setup.
We already use HubSpot or another CRM. Do we need to switch?
Not necessarily. We review your current setup and recommend the best path; if you like, we can also plan a migration to CRM Corner, integrated with Apollo, LinkedIn and Gmail. For a CRM comparison, see our Zoho vs. Pipedrive guide (in Turkish).
Can you reach customers abroad as well?
Yes. We work with more than 100 partners in over 5 countries and design outbound campaigns around your target market.

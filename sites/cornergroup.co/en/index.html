About Us

What is Corner Group?

Corner Group is a B2B growth ecosystem running R&D at Kocaeli Teknopark. Its five brands work as one team: SAAS Corner finds new B2B customers through outbound sales, Sales Corner builds sales automation, CRM Corner offers a locally built CRM that brings sales and marketing into a single flow, Social Media Corner handles social media and ad management, and Brand Corner focuses on influencer marketing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does Corner Group offer?
With five brands, we cover the end-to-end needs of B2B growth: outbound sales and lead generation (SAAS Corner), sales automation (Sales Corner), CRM (CRM Corner), social media management (Social Media Corner) and influencer marketing (Brand Corner). You can use the brands individually or together.
How do I know which Corner is right for me?
With the Stack Builder on the home page, you can pick the Corners you need and request a custom quote. If you're not sure, we'll work out together which Corners will benefit you in a free 30-minute discovery call.
Which CRMs and tools do you work with?
CRM Corner is a Teknopark-approved, locally built CRM that brings your sales and marketing processes into a single flow, with Apollo, LinkedIn and Gmail integrations. On the automation side we work with n8n and AI tools. To compare different CRMs, see our Zoho vs. Pipedrive comparison and our HubSpot pipeline setup guide (in Turkish).
How long does setup take?
The timeline depends on your industry, team size and existing infrastructure, and the roadmap is tailored to your company. In a typical engagement the first Corner infrastructure is in place by month 3, the system settles in by month 6 and moves forward with weekly iterations, and scaling begins in year 1.
How is pricing determined?
Instead of fixed package prices, we prepare a custom quote based on the Corners you choose and your goals. After the discovery call we share a customized roadmap.
How do you measure and report results?
We report weekly and optimize monthly. With open reporting every month and a strategy review every quarter, we keep the process transparent and data-driven.
Which industries do you serve?
We work with B2B companies in software and SaaS, e-commerce, agencies, finance, education, industry and export, and healthcare. We describe our industry-specific approach on our SaaS, manufacturing and export and e-commerce pages.

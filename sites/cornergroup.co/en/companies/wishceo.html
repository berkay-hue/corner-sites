WishCEO: you make the call

WishCEO is a CEO game published issue by issue, built on cases adapted from real business crises. You read the file, make your decision, then see what the CEO did that day and how it ended. One file takes about three minutes.

Visit website: wishceo.com ↗
Who it serves

Who it serves

Anyone curious about hard business decisions who enjoys thinking about strategy. No account is needed; the game runs in the browser.

What it does

Highlights

Cases from real events

Company, product and people names are changed and figures rounded; the subject is the decision, not the brand.

Short files

Figures and context take two minutes to read; you pick one of four options.

CEO report card

Each issue ends with a report card based on your decisions that you can compare with friends.

Archive and alerts

Past issues stay in the archive, and you can get an email when a new issue is out.

Frequently asked questions

What is WishCEO?
WishCEO is a browser game published issue by issue, where players make the CEO's decision in cases adapted from real business crises.
Are the cases real?
They are adapted from real events with company, product and people names changed. After your decision you see what happened.
Do I need an account?
No. The game is free to play without an account; you can optionally sign up for new-issue emails.

Sales Corner: the studio that automates sales

Sales Corner is a sales automation agency that automates B2B sales processes with AI and n8n. It hands repetitive work, from finding leads and preparing meeting briefs to analysing sales calls and drafting proposals, to AI-powered workflows.

Visit website: salescorner.co ↗
Who it serves

Who it serves

B2B companies whose sales teams lose time to manual research, data entry and reporting. A fit for sales and operations teams that want ready-made automations or workflows built around their own tool stack.

What it does

Highlights

Five-layer architecture

Every workflow runs through trigger, data enrichment, AI layer, action and logging steps.

Pre-meeting briefs

Gathers company and contact data before a call and delivers a ready brief.

Sales call analysis

Scores recorded meetings against sales methodologies and writes coaching notes.

Custom tech stack

Connects Apollo, Google Workspace, your CRM and LLMs the way your company works.

Frequently asked questions

What does Sales Corner do?
Sales Corner is a sales automation agency that automates B2B sales processes with AI and n8n workflows.
Which tools does Sales Corner use?
Its website describes workflows built with n8n, Apollo, Google Workspace, Claude, Gemini and GPT.
How is Sales Corner different from SAAS Corner?
SAAS Corner runs outbound with a human SDR team; Sales Corner automates the sales team's repetitive work. They can be used together.
